# shadow

## Trigger

The `shadow` error reports a literal densely copied bare from one source root.

## Move

Read the source and destination responsibilities. If the destination is a projection, derive or compose it from the source and delete the hand-maintained bridge. If a real layer border requires independent translation, keep the bridge only after local judgment and declare the boundary explicitly.

## Evidence

The pre-P1 keel config bridge copied four of five fields and disappeared when one cascade and derived model replaced the twins; the fleet calibration kept honest layer translations below the alarm.

## EXIT

Remove this entry when the shadow verdict becomes mechanical or the shadow law retires.
